Privacy

Privacy notice

For hushlantern.com, the waitlist and the game. Last updated October 23, 2026.

Hushlantern Games ("we") makes games for kids, teens and adults, so we collect as little as we can and explain it plainly. This notice covers hushlantern.com, the waitlist and the game at hushlantern.com/play.

The short version

  • No ads, no selling data, no advertising trackers, no cookies.
  • Accounts belong to a grown-up. Children play under a nickname and never give us their name or email.
  • We count visits, sign-ins and time spent with our own privacy-friendly tool so we can improve the game. We don't store IP addresses with it.

Visiting the website

We record which pages are viewed, roughly how long they stay open, the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer), the website that sent you (if any) and the page language. A random visit number is kept in your browser tab's temporary storage and is deleted when you close the tab. It can't follow you to other websites. We use no cookies.

Our web host (GitHub Pages), database provider (Supabase) and font provider (Google Fonts) receive standard technical information such as your IP address when your browser contacts them, as any website's providers do. We don't add IP addresses to our own records.

The waitlist

Only what you type in: your email address, optionally who will play (age groups such as "Ages 8–10" or "My class"), your country (US, Canada or elsewhere), the language, the date and your confirmation that you're 18 or older. We use it only to email you about the launch and, if you ticked "My class", about classroom use.

Game accounts

The account holder: a parent or guardian, or a player aged 13 or over, signs in with an email address. We send a one-time sign-in link to that address; there is no password to remember. We keep the email address to sign you in and, when there are players under 13, as the parent's contact.

Player profiles: for each player we keep a nickname (chosen from fun options such as "Brave Owl"), the month and year of birth (to choose age-right cases, never the full date), region and language settings, music and sharing settings, and game progress such as solved cases, hints and points. A parent PIN protects parent settings; we keep only a scrambled check value, never the PIN itself.

Permission for under-13s: players under 13 (or the age set by local law) can only be added by a signed-in parent or guardian, who confirms they agree. We keep a record of that confirmation.

Playing with friends is off for under-13s unless a parent turns it on. Friends join a private table with a 4-letter code. Players at the table see each other's nicknames, positions in the scene, clues found and votes. There is no text or voice chat. Table data is deleted 7 days after the game.

Guest play: you can play without an account. Guest progress stays in your browser on that device.

How we measure the game

To keep the game running well and decide what to build next, the game records events such as signing in, opening and solving a case, wrong guesses, hints used, and time spent playing. Each event carries the account's internal ID, a random player ID and an age group (for example "8–10"), but never a nickname, name or email. This information is used only inside Hushlantern, is never used for advertising and is never shared to build profiles of children.

Who can see it

Only the Hushlantern team. Everything is stored with Supabase, which processes it for us under its data processing terms. The database only lets each account read its own saves, and nobody outside our team can read the waitlist or the measurement records. We don't sell, rent or share personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Website and game measurement records: 13 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Accounts, profiles and progress: until you delete them or ask us to.
  • Waitlist: until launch and up to 12 months after, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Multiplayer tables: 7 days.

Children

We designed the game for children from age 8, with a parent in charge of the account. We don't ask children for their name, email, photos, contacts or location, and we don't show ads. If you think a child has given us information without permission, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can remove a player profile in the game (Parent settings, with your PIN) or sign out at any time. To see, correct or delete everything we hold about you or your child, write to hello@hushlantern.com. Parents can also ask us to stop collecting information from their child. Depending on where you live (for example under COPPA in the US, or PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25 in Canada), you may have further rights, and we'll honor them.

Contact

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you already gave us, we'll ask first.
